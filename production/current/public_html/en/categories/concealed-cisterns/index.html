Choosing your product

Which concealed cistern suits my project?

Identify the toilet type, installation conditions and available space within the wall. Check the selected model's dimensions and installation requirements against the project with your installer. Appearance alone is not enough to choose.

Will any flush button work with any cistern?

No. Check the cistern and button models together for their connection and mechanism. Do not choose a button on colour or shape alone. Provide both exact model names to confirm compatibility before ordering.

How can I explore models and enquire about an order?

Open the model you like to see its photographs and details. Leave your email or phone number in the product inquiry section to ask about finishes and order details.
