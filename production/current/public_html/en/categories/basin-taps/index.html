Choosing your product

How do I match a basin tap to my washbasin?

Check the mounting position, basin rim height and where the spout reaches into the bowl together. Ask about the basin and tap models as a pair so dimensions and installation can be checked before ordering.

Should I choose a standard or a tall basin tap?

The choice depends on the basin shape and tap mounting position, not appearance alone. Compare standard and tall models against the bowl height and available space. Ask for help with your intended combination if you need more dimensions.

How can I explore models and enquire about an order?

Open the model you like to see its photographs and details. Leave your email or phone number in the product inquiry section to ask about finishes and order details. If you have not chosen a model yet, start with our contact page. Explore these models to get started: Albite Basin Tap · Albite Plus Basin Tap. Related collection to explore: Tall basin taps.
