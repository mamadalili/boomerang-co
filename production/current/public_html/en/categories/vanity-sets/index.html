Choosing your product

How do I choose a vanity set for a small bathroom?

Measure the available width and depth, including clearance for doors and drawers, and compare them with the chosen model. Check plumbing positions and circulation space too. Ask us before ordering if a required dimension is not shown.

How do I check what comes with a vanity set?

Read the components section and description of the specific model. Check the cabinet, basin, mirror and accessories individually. A tap or accessory appearing in a photograph does not by itself mean it is included in the order.

How can I explore models and enquire about an order?

Open the model you like to see its photographs and details. Leave your email or phone number in the product inquiry section to ask about finishes and order details. If you have not chosen a model yet, start with our contact page. Explore these models to get started: Amanda 60 Wall-Mounted · Andre 47 Wall-Mounted. Related collection to explore: Mirrors.
