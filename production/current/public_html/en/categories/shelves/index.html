Choosing your product

How do I choose a shelf for my bathroom?

Consider the available wall width, usable depth and the items you want to place on it. Choose a position that does not obstruct movement or opening doors, and check the model's dimensions before ordering.

What should I check before installing a shelf?

Discuss the wall material, fixing method and intended load with your installer. Ask about the fixing requirements and load limit for the specific model rather than judging from a photograph or a similar-looking shelf.

How can I explore models and enquire about an order?

Open the model you like to see its photographs and details. Leave your email or phone number in the product inquiry section to ask about finishes and order details.
