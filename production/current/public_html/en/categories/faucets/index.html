Choosing your product

Should I choose a faucet set or individual taps?

Explore sets if you want a coordinated design and finish. If you are replacing only one tap, start with its intended use, such as kitchen or basin taps, and check the installation position too.

Which items are included in a faucet set?

Read the component list on the specific set's page and match each item's use to your needs. Ask separately about showers, risers, hoses and installation accessories. A collection photograph alone does not establish that every item is included.

How can I explore models and enquire about an order?

Open the model you like to see its photographs and details. Leave your email or phone number in the product inquiry section to ask about finishes and order details. If you have not chosen a model yet, start with our contact page. Explore these models to get started: Albite 4-Piece Faucet Set · Albite Plus 4-Piece Faucet Set.
