Family design

Seven uses angular lines and a visually distinctive body for contemporary interiors. The proportions of its components keep the kitchen, basin, bath and toilet taps within one design language.

Composition and use

This set is intended for choosing one model across four water-use points. Its defined components and listed technical specifications help you assess the combination for each space.
