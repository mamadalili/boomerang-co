TAREN 44 · COMPACT WALL-MOUNTED VANITY

Taren 44: a compact wall-mounted set by Boomerang with a Corian basin and vertical Clean mirror

Taren 44, made by Boomerang, consists of a Taren wall-mounted cabinet, Taren washbasin in Corian and Clean mirror. Its 43 cm body, 44 cm basin and elongated 72 cm mirror give the set distinct proportions.

The cabinet measures 43 × 30 × 40 cm; the Taren basin in Corian measures 44 × 30 × 14 cm. The basin is one centimetre longer, while the 30 cm dimension matches both parts.

For Taren, “44” is more than a model label: basin length is actually 44 cm, while the body is 43 cm. Include the small difference in precise installation measurements.

Three measurements for understanding Taren

Cabinet 43 cm, basin 44 cm, mirror height 72 cm

43: cabinet length

44: length of the Corian basin

72: height of the Clean mirror

These measurements show how Taren occupies the wall more clearly than its name. The basin area needs about 44 cm horizontally; the 72 cm high mirror creates a taller vertical element.

A practical point about Taren 44

Cabinet and basin match in the 30 cm dimension

A simpler footprint to measure

Cabinet: 43 × 30 cm; basin: 44 × 30 cm. The 30 cm dimension matches; the main difference is one centimetre in length.

For tight spaces, use the basin as the final boundary

If Taren is near a side wall, frame or other fitting, use the 44 × 30 cm basin footprint to check the actual upper clearance.

The Clean mirror: the vertical element

A small cabinet with a distinct 72 cm vertical mirror

The Clean mirror, 40 × 72 cm, is vertical. Plan not only cabinet space but enough height above the basin for the mirror and users' field of view.

Align the mirror centre with the basin. Set the distance above it using users' height, taps and lighting. The mirror is part of Taren's visual composition and should not be positioned separately at the last stage.

Taren washbasin

This set specifies a Taren basin in Corian, rather than a porcelain basin

The Taren basin is made of Corian, measuring 44 × 30 × 14 cm. When comparing Taren with sets using sanitary porcelain basins, consider material as well as size.

Colours of Taren

From White and Grey to Cream and Sky blue

Recorded colours for Taren: Cream, Grey, Sky blue and White. Alongside neutral colours, Sky blue broadens the palette. Choose with bathroom lighting, tiles and taps in mind.
