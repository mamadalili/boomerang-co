Lisano 60 · Bathroom mirror

Boomerang Lisano 60 vanity mirror: choose with its actual 63 cm length in mind

The Boomerang Lisano 60 vanity mirror is named in the Pito 60 set, and its recorded actual dimensions are 63 × 1.6 × 58 cm. The number 60 in the model name must therefore not be taken as the mirror's exact width; its 63 cm length matters when checking wall space.

This page concerns choosing Lisano 60 independently. If you already have the cabinet and basin, match the space above them to the mirror's actual dimensions and choose it separately from a complete set.

Size and selection

Lisano 60: note the difference between the size name and actual dimensions

When positioning Lisano 60, check the recorded length of 63 cm, the 1.6 cm dimension and the 58 cm height. Assess clearances near walls, tile edges and lights using these dimensions.

In the original Pito 60 combination, the cabinet and mirror have similar size names, but their dimensions differ. This does not prevent coordination; establish the mirror's position relative to the basin centre and installation space before ordering.

Lisano 60 · Colour selection

Coordinating Lisano 60 with Pito depends on the chosen sample

Check the frame colour against the sample being ordered and the description of that option; do not assume that Pito's cabinet colour is the mirror colour.

When comparing appearance, consider the bathroom lighting and wall colour. A complete-set photograph helps show the mirror's proportions relative to the basin, but colour confirmation must concern the mirror itself.

Use and independent selection

The Lisano mirror with Pito or an existing vanity

If you have chosen Pito 60 for your bathroom, Lisano 60 is the mirror named as a complementary option for that set. For a standalone order, check the mirror sample and colour separately from the cabinet colour.

When replacing an old mirror, compare its previous width with 63 cm and recheck the fixing positions. The model name 60 alone does not establish suitability for a 60 cm space.

To see the documented combination of this mirror with other components, refer to the Pito 60 wall-mounted vanity set. These specifications concern the standalone mirror.

Lisano 60's presence in the Pito set makes it possible to examine the relationship between the two components through Boomerang's official store. Boomerang's manufacturing business and official sales channel support this supply, allowing the mirror and set specifications to be checked through one channel.
