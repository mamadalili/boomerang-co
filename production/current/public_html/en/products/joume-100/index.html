JOUME 100 WALL MOUNTED

Joume 100: a wall-mounted vanity set made by Boomerang

Joume 100 is a wall-mounted vanity set made by Boomerang. It combines a 100 cm long cabinet, Murano 90 mirror and Santa 100 washbasin for everyday bathroom use.

The set includes a 100 × 47 × 36 cm cabinet, Murano 90 mirror and washbasin in sanitary porcelain. Each component is specified for this model in the Boomerang catalogue.

Boomerang manufactures vanity cabinets and related bathroom products. The wall-mounted Joume 100 has defined technical specifications and its own model identity.
